Points per game in top European football leagues 2015-2016

November 13, 2015

Points per game from top leagues in Europe so far this season

Inspired by a visualization on twittersphere, I decided to make my own javascript version of the points per game chart. The thing that stands out for me is how, even after one-third of the season barely gone, the race for the top spot in Bundesliga and League 1 is all but over. Things look bleaky for Troyes in League 1 and it does not look good for Capri and Verona in Serie A either. Barring the top two, Bundesliga looks pretty competitive. I guess they should start having a trophy for third place.
