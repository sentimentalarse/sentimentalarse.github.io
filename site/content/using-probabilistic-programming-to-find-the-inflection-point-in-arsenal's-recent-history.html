Using probabilistic programming to find the inflection point in Arsenal's recent history

October 28, 2015

Can we use probabilistic programming to prove Wenger's revolution?

Everbody and their dog who follows Arsenal knows that Arsene Wenger revolutionized the way Arsenal play the game. So how does this post end up being about Arsene and probabilistic programming? Well, I was going through an excellent book "Probabilistic Programming and Bayesian Methods for Hackers" and was curious about where I could use the modeling "expertize" I gained from Chapter 1. It just so happens that I recently found a great data source for English soccer results from 1871-2014 and me being SentimentalArse, decided to use Arsenal results to prove once and for all that Arsene Wenger indeed revolutionized Arsenal's game.

Moving into the modeling part, I created a model similar to the one used in example "Inferring behaviour from text-message data" but replaced message counts with goals scored by Arsenal (I only considered games starting from 1986/01/01 and onwards). The result from the model shows the inflection point gradually starting from August 1996 and reaching its peak in around January 1999. Here is what it looks like. You can clearly see a bump around January, 1999.
