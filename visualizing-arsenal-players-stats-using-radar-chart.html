Visualizing player performance using radar chart

The inspiration for this post comes from this awesome whiskey flavor profile chart and the great work by Nadieh Bremer. Radar chart is a great way to visualize multivariate data in two-dimensions and the chart below is an attempt at summarizing the defensive and offensive performances of players compared to the top performers in the team. By the way, the data was collected from Whoscored.com and is up to date until December 15th 2015.
